Who We Are

Your Plug for Tasty African Dishes

New Haven Restaurant is a celebration of African flavours — bold, soulful, and unapologetically homemade. Nestled at Atlanta Mall along Kirichwa Road, we bring the taste of the motherland right to your table.

From smoked beef stews and goat choma to coconut fish and kienyeji chicken, every dish is cooked with fire, love, and a deep respect for tradition.
